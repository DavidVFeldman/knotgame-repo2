# Proposition 9 (the plastic number) — certified

Paper reference: Proposition 9 of the compiled PDF, `\label{prop:plastic}`
(the commission's "Proposition 3.4").

> For `λ = ρ` the orbit `Orb(ρ)` has 153 points, there are 25 525 reachable
> configurations, and `sup_n N_ρ(n) = 7`. The least lengths attaining `k` knots
> for `k ≤ 6` are 1, 3, 5, 7, 12, 17.

**All four clauses are now certified**, sorry-free, with kernel `decide` only —
no `native_decide`, no new axioms. This supersedes the negative report of round 1
(`PLASTIC-REPORT.md`), which stands as the record of why the first route failed
and of the cost measurements that were made then.

## How the certificate works

The paper's verification is a breadth-first search. Replaying a search inside
the kernel is what failed in round 1. Instead the search is run outside Lean
and its *result* is shipped as data, with the kernel checking a small number of
closure properties which imply the claims. Nothing about the way the data was
produced is trusted.

Each node of the tree `V` carries a key, a configuration `c` (a sorted list of
orbit indices), a tag `d`, and a move `pm` with a configuration `pc`. The kernel
checks, at every node (`chkV`, `okV`, `sizeV`, `nil_mem_V`):

1. **Closure.** Each of `stepIdx L c`, `stepIdx M c`, `stepIdx R c` is found in
   `V` with a tag at most `d + 1`.
2. **Size.** `|c| ≤ 7`, and `|c| ≥ k` forces `d ≥ 1, 3, 5, 7, 12, 17, 28` for
   `k = 1, …, 7`.
3. **Reachability.** Either `d = 0` and `c` is empty, or `pc` is found in `V`
   with tag at most `d - 1` and `stepIdx (moveOf pm) pc = c`.
4. **Shape.** The tree is a search tree for its keys, each key being `encCfg`
   of the configuration stored with it, and it has 25 525 nodes.

Checks 1 and 2 give, by induction along a word, that `runIdx w` is in `V` with
a tag at most `|w|`; hence `≤ 7` knots always, and `≥ k` knots only after the
stated number of moves. Check 3 gives, by induction on the tag, that everything
in `V` is reachable. Check 4 turns "the reachable configurations are exactly the
contents of `V`" into the count 25 525: the keys along the in-order traversal
are strictly increasing, so the stored configurations are distinct, and the node
count is computed without ever materialising a 25 525-element list in the
kernel.

Soundness does not depend on check 4: lookups compare the stored configuration
honestly (`decide (c' = c)`), so a badly keyed tree could only make a lookup
*fail*. Check 4 is needed only for the converse — that every stored
configuration can be found — and for the count.

Two design points make the kernel cost tolerable:

* **Byte-packed transition tables.** `tabL`, `tabM`, `tabR` are single natural
  numbers with one byte per orbit index (255 meaning "the knot dies"), and
  `tget T i = (T >>> (8*i)) % 256` is a GMP shift, not a list traversal. The
  tables are themselves checked against the certified survival and action
  predicates on `ℤ[ρ]`-triples (`chk_tab`).
* **Bounded recursion depth.** Every traversal in the check recurses on the
  *tree* (depth ≈ 15 for 25 525 balanced nodes), never on a long list. The
  earlier prototype that folded over a 25 525-element list crashed the kernel.

Two orbit points sit exactly on a deletion threshold — `orbitList[58] = r/2` and
`orbitList[94] = 1 − r/2`. Survival being strict, both die under `M`; the
comparisons in `PlasticIndex.lean` are therefore three-valued (`<`, `>`, or an
exact equality of coordinate triples).

## The orbit count

`PlasticOrbit.lean` (round 2) certifies that the orbit is *contained* in the
153-point list. `PlasticOrbitCount.lean` supplies the converse: for each index,
a record `(distance from 1/2, move, predecessor index)`, checked by the kernel
over `List.range 153`. Induction on the distance produces, for every listed
point, an explicit word carrying `1/2` to it. Hence `Orb ρ` equals the listed
set, and the 153 listed points are distinct because consecutive ones differ by
at least `239/100000` (round 2).

## Scruples

* **The count is of configurations as sets of knots.** `card_reachable_configs`
  counts the distinct values of `run ρ w` over all words `w`, as elements of
  `Finset ℝ`; the empty configuration (the value at `w = []`) is one of the
  25 525. The index-model count `card_reachable_idx` is the same number, and the
  two are identified by the injectivity of `cfgSet` on sorted index lists.
* **`d ρ k` is the paper's least length** in the sense of the development's
  `d λ k = sInf {n | k ≤ N λ n}`, i.e. the least length of a word producing `k`
  simultaneous knots.
* **The certificate is generated data.** `RequestProject/PlasticCert.lean` is
  machine-written (1.4 MB) by `scripts/plastic_cert.py`, which reads the
  transition tables out of `PlasticIndex.lean`, so the shipped data and the data
  the kernel checks against are the same. Regenerating it reproduces the file.
* **What is still not claimed.** The smallest gap of the orbit is bounded below
  by `239/100000` (round 2), not computed exactly; the paper's `≈ 0.00239`
  agrees with the true value but is not certified as such.
